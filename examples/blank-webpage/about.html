Our Story

A galley built for travelers, by travelers.

How we set sail

The Thousand Sunny Galley began as a pop-up aboard a chartered sloop in the East Blue, serving black-leg pasta to crews who couldn't agree on a restaurant. The pop-up kept sailing. Five years and three ports later, we cast a permanent anchor on Calm Belt and opened the doors for good. We still cook the way Sanji taught us: one plate at a time, no shortcuts, every guest a nakama. The sea is wide, the menu is long, and there's always a chair waiting for you.
